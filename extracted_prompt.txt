Member: Read-only access to their profile, club members list, speech progress, meeting schedules, assigned roles, attendance record, mentor info, meeting agendas; personal sign out.

Excel Export: SheetJS export generating PSS_Miyapur_Gavel_Club_Members_YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx containing Member ID, Full Name, Mobile, Class/Year, School/College, Branch, Mentor, Speeches Completed, Attendance %, Registration Date, Status. Supports exporting all, by branch, or by filtered search results.
